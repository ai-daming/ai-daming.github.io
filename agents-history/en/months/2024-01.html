01-16, paper v1

AlphaCodium proposes a test-centric, multi-stage, iterative remediation process; reporting CodeContests validation set GPT-4 pass@5 from 19% for a single prompt to 44% for the process.

public node · A reminder to readers: Process design beyond models has directly improved code results before "Agent" became a popular product name. It is about competition questions and author experiments, not about maintaining the completion rate of the entire code base.

01-25, actual measurement by the author

Aider tested both old and new Turbos and found that the new model wasn't better across the board on its lazy/refactoring benchmark, with results affected by the editing format.

public node · Good to pair with the previous point: "model upgrade" does not automatically equal "your agent workflow is better". Extrapolation can only be made to this test design and it cannot be said that the new GPT-4 is degraded for all users.
