03-19

METR proposes to measure the Agent's ability by the time it takes humans to complete it. The original study pointed out that a 50% success rate corresponds to a doubling of task time approximately every 7 months. This indicator expands "how many questions are answered correctly" to "how long the task can be independently undertaken"; it is not a guarantee of the autonomy time for actual projects.
