2025-04

Direct event

04-09

Google released A2A to solve communication and task collaboration between different Agents; it is different from the tool/context access layer of MCP and is a cross-system interoperability direction. It cannot be written that collaboration problems have been universally eliminated.

public node · See original source text for status and restrictions.

Original source 1

Human–agent collaboration that month (industry-wide)

People are responsible for business discovery, architecture, and handover. Web prototypes cannot replace the establishment of requirements.

Read and write warehouses and execute codes independently; cross-agent communication standards are proposed, and non-coding delivery is checked according to tasks
